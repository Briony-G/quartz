This is the prosperous, hopeful kingdom of trade, culture and magic on the east of the continent. Its peoples celebrate life with festivals and music. It is the place most of our adventures have taken place.

It consists of [[Upper Ayderon]] and [[Lower Ayderon]].

##### Notable locations here:
[[Paulderburg]] - the largest city in [[Ayderon]]. Capital of the [[Upper Ayderon]] region, it is built atop the ruins of an ancient elvish city.
[[Tideholm Keep]] - a castle off the coast of [[Paulderburg]], granted to Kathryn’s Angels as reward for their heroic deeds.
[[Blackwater]] - Capital of the [[Lower Ayderon]] region, built on stilts atop the lake that gives the city it’s name.
[[Aldarfort]] - City in [[Lower Ayderon]]. Ruled by the good [[Lady Kathryn]], ally of Kathryn’s Angels. 
[[Throlor]] - A [[Dwarf]]hold built into the most northern mountain in the Black Ridge. Rich in gold and mithril ore. 
[[Legnipper’s Dreadfort]] - An old [[Dwarf|Dwarven]] fortress lost in the great goblin war. [[Goblin]]s now live here and emulate their twisted idea of [[Human]] civilisation.
[[Feymoot]] - A new settlement for [[Fey|faerie]] refugees, funded by [[Lady Kathryn]]. Locally renowned for its theatres, strip clubs, minstrels and jesters.
