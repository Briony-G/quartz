Is a dark weapon forged by the Archmage of Abomination, [[Wrenndel Trollenharm]]. A strike from this blade leaves wounds no magic can heal, its edge imbued with necromantic energy. 

One of the 8 items from [[The Loom Weave of Eightfold]].
