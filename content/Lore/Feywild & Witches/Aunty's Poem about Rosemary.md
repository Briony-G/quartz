> [!warning] [[Aunty]]'s Poem about [[Rosemary]]:
> 6 years to grow,
> 6 months to bloom,
> 6 days to break the cradle's womb.
> 
> 7 teeth sold,
> A lover's face lost
> shall bear bitter fruit
> when the Rose pays the cost.
> 
> When [[Baba Yaga|Baba]] seeps
> into her new daughter's flesh,
> the witch-queen shall rise
> and grandmama loses her bet.
> ^Poem
