Year: 540-567PU

The Verdanian Conflict was a prolonged and bitter war between the kingdoms of [[Ayderon]] and [[Verdania]], driven by long-standing rivalries and territorial ambitions. The conflict ravaged the continent, with battles such as the [[Battle of the Rosemarches]] and the creation of [[The Blasted Lands]] serving as grim reminders of the war's devastation. The use of powerful magic on both sides left the land scarred and desolate, particularly in regions like the Rosemarches and [[The Blasted Lands]]. Although a temporary ceasefire was established with the Treaty of the Twin Rivers in 565PU, the war ultimately concluded with the Fall of Verdania in 567PU, when the kingdom's capital, [[Norrendorf]], was besieged and [[King Eadric III]] was killed. Verdania's collapse marked the end of an era and the fragmentation of the once-proud kingdom. ^TheVerdanianConflict
