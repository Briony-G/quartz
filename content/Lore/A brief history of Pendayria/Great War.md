Year: 520-527PU

The Great War, also known as the [[Goblin]] War, was a devastating conflict that erupted when the [[Goblin]] clans of [[Upper Ayderon]], united under the fearsome warlord [[Legnipper]], launched a series of brutal attacks on the surrounding regions. The [[Goblin]]s overran villages, slaughtering the inhabitants and eventually capturing a dwarven citadel, which they transformed into their stronghold, [[Legnipper’s Dreadfort]]. The war raged for seven years, with the combined forces of [[Paulderburg]] and [[Throlor]] struggling to repel the [[Goblin]] invaders. Finally, the [[Goblin]] forces were defeated and driven back into the mountains, but the conflict left deep wounds and a lingering fear of a resurgence. ^TheGreatWar
