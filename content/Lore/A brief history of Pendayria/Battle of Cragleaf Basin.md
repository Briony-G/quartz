The Battle of Cragleaf Basin stands as the bloodiest and most devastating conflict in the known history of [[Pendayria]]. Sparked by a failed trade deal between the [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] of [[Throlor]] and the [[Wood elf|Wood Elves]] of [[Athelthorne]], the battle escalated into a full-scale war. The [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], with their [[Gnome|gnomish]] allies, unleashed powerful explosive devices that wreaked havoc on the [[Elf|elven]] forces, resulting in massive casualties. In a desperate act, the [[Elf|elven]] sorcerers called upon ancient gods, casting a petrification spell that turned the entire battlefield, including the combatants and the land itself, to stone. This grim tableau still stands as a haunting reminder of the futility of war, and the truce that followed has remained a tenuous peace ever since. ^TheBattleOfCrafleafBasin
