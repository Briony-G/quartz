### Rohan's Summary

The morning after your night in the tavern, you discussed your next plans. An ugly stereotype of a witch, named [[Swagra]], offered to be your guide to [[Motherhorn Castle]]. You declined. Instead, choosing to travel with a [[Tiefling]] in an oversized knitted sweater- [[Salma]]. 🧙 😈 🧶 
  
You cleverly noticed that [[Aunty]] appeared to be going by an other name here, [[Aunty|Weatherspoon]]. you pieced together that maybe this related to her having some power over the weather in the region, (side note- back in [[Brine]]'s realm- you remember that the frog people worshipped [[Brine]] almost as a hero, after she saved them from a storm that [[Aunty]] allegedly sent out to them. 

[[Fin]] was nowhere to be found in the treasure trail in the morning. she needed some space to clear her head and agreed to catch up to the party in a few hours. 

You chose to travel through the hollows, an underground cave system through the caverns of the purple crystals, where you encountered stalactites that dripped acid, and every echo turned into applause. 

You realised that this cavern was seeped in repressed, unhappiness. purple crystals grew out of the walls in the cavern, you recalled these as [[mnemosyne stones]] - a rare crystal that forms only in specific regions in the feywild. They form in places of deep emotional turmoil, or when someone focuses on a specific memory that is very emotional to them. 

[[Ellywick]] played her saddest song, about not being able to save a halfling from the goblins at the start of your adventure, and a crystal formed. [[Ellywick]] felt all of her emotions surrounding that incident, leave her body. like she was indifferent to that memory now.

She smashed the crystal and you all saw a purple ghost like vision of [[Ellywick]] sing the same song she just played, like a ghostly recording, and you all suddenly felt a deep pang of grief and sorrow, before the recording finished and the feelings left.

[[Beet]] realised she could see the ghostly faces of people inside the crystals, and with the help of the party, found three crystals that had [[Aunty]]'s face in them, and  you carefully harvested the stones, unsure how to activate them without smashing them.

Memories:
- [[Aunty]] pacing in her boudoir, voice low
- [[Aunty]] as a much younger girl, with another girl in the memory. [[Aunty]] is telling the girl to sit still
- [[Aunty]] and [[Yassandra]], as young girls still. Pre-surgery. They appear excited.

You traveled deeper and found a subterranean lake, with an island in it, where you met [[Uni]], the unicorn. Last of her herd, she warned you not to enter the lake. You introduced yourselves to her and learnt that she was guarded this lake, and that it was created from her tears. She was the last of her kind, her herd hunted to near extinction-primarily from [[Aunty]]'s poaching.  

You learnt that someone can give up pieces of their memory altogether. As a sign of good faith, Ivy gave up a memory of one day in her cabin in [[Athelthorne]], and told [[Uni]] that she would be happier there- [[Beet]] remembered that other unicorns also lived in [[Athelthorne]] so she wouldn't be alone.

[[Uni]] showed the party one memory that [[Aunty]] had given up, and forgotten. it showed a teenage [[Aunty]], talking to herself. claiming that she had seen the future, and in it, [[Yassandra]] becomes so obsessed with perfection, she would be a terrible mother and eat her own young. she said she would make herself the villain to save [[Yassandra]]'s future child. she then convinced the young [[Yassandra]] into making this bargain, trading her first born in return for alchemical powers and beauty. 

[[Uni]] appreciated fine art, so [[Beet]] drew her a picture of a unicorn in the mud at the riverbank, [[Salma]] used her elemental magics to animate the unicorns and make the painting jump about, [[Uni]] enjoyed this and brought the party a gift, the horn of her mate. 

[[Ellywick]] identified that this horn had 3 charges, and could be used to end sicknesses, dispel curses, lycanthropy, vampirisms, and some other abilities. It was a powerful item and sure enough to be needed in the future. 
  
After leaving the caverns, you found yourself at the graveyard of lost acts, where a pagaent wagon trundled up to you and small marionettes that looked like yourselves flittered about on a stage. [[Ellywick]] touched the wagon and it immediately fell apart into loose planks and painted wood.

Around you, there were tombstones in elvish, with the names of musicians and performers that had not given a good enough performance.

You defaced some of the tombstones and wrote [[Aunty]]'s name on them, almost immediately, bolts of lighting shot down from the sky and attempted to smote you. 

You left on, and continued up [[Motherhorn Castle]], getting closer and closer to the peak to prepare for your roast…..
