*requires attunement*
Forged from the rare metal blavistite (from [[Nodium Vale]]), this dark heavy gauntlet hums with a faint, dissonant vibration, distorting magical energy around it.

**Mage Breaker** - when unarmed strike a creature concentrating on a spell, they have disadvantage on saving throw to not break concentration

**Spell Breaker** - advantage on saving throws to resist spell effects

**Arcanist's bane** - when grapple a creature, creature can't cast spells or activate magical items
