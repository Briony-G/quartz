##### **Shape of the Horned Goat** (can use once per long rest)
- As an action, you may transform into a black horned goat for up to 1 hour, or until you dismiss the effect.
Functions similarly to wild shape, with following rules:
- Use stats of a goat, except: keep Wisdom, Intelligence and Charisma scores the same, and alignment, personality and memories are retained.
