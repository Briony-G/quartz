Pendayria is a world shaped by old powers, ancient stone and the tension between what was and what is becoming. Two nations define the current age:
1. [[Ayderon]], the prosperous, hopeful kingdom of trade, culture and magic on the east of the continent. Its peoples celebrate life with festivals and music. It is the place most of our adventures have taken place.
2. [[Verdania]], is a colder, harsher land to the west. Once powerful, now worn down by wars. Its people are practical and proud and suspicious of magic, especially after the devastating witchhunts of yore.

**Other key locations:**
	[[The Astronomica]] - Due to a magical phenomenon, a series of mountain peaks were severed and began to float in the sky. On one of these, is the Astronomica, a repurposed [[Dwarf|Dwarven]] fortress that now serves as the tower of an Archmage.
	[[Athelthorne (city)]] - The [[Wood elf]] capital, sharing the same name as the forest it is in.
	[[Maenshar]] - The [[High Elf]] capital of the Maenshari Archipelago. It is a magical city of towering glass, gems and coral.
	[[Dwandelheim]] - The oldest [[Dwarf]]hold. Behind its heavy gates, it protects some of the most ancient runes and spells in the land.
