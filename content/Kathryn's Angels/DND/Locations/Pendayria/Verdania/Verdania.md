This is the colder, harsher land to the west of [[Pendayria]]. Once powerful, now worn down by wars. Its people are practical and proud and suspicious of magic, especially after the devastating witchhunts of yore.

Magic is illegal here and punishable by death.

Most Notable locations:
	[[Norrendorf]] - Capital of [[Verdania]]. Mysterious and corrupt, its streets lined with anti-magic propaganda.
	[[Cliffkeep]] - A [[Dwarf]]hold on the eastern border. Older than the kingdom of [[Verdania]], it is built on tradition, industry and honour.
	[[Sylroot]] - A [[Gnome|gnomish]] village hidden deep within the Sageweald forest.
	[[The Maven Academy]] - Protected from the rest of [[Verdania]] by the wild magic that still lingers in [[The Blasted Lands]], the Academy is a school for witches.
	[[Convent of Still Dawn]] - An ancient monkish hermitage high in the Stormpeak Mountains. Here, worshippers lead semi-solitary lives steeped in tradition.
