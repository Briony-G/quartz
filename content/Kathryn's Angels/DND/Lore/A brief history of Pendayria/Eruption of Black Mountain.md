Year: 630PU

The Eruption of Black Mountain in 630PU was a cataclysmic event that shook the entire continent. When a Red Dragon made its lair inside the dormant volcano, it unknowingly awakened a powerful fire elemental. In a display of power, the elemental caused the volcano to erupt, spewing ash and lava across [[Lower Ayderon]]. The skies darkened as ash clouds spread, and the [[Blackwater]] Lake began to boil. The eruption wreaked havoc across the region until a group of adventurers defeated the fire elemental, returning the mountain to dormancy. However, the eruption left lasting scars on the land, and the legend persists that a portal to the [[Plane of Fire]] still exists within the volcano's depths. ^EruptionBlackMountain
