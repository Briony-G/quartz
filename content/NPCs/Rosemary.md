[[Ivy]]'s surrogate child

stolen by [[Baba Yaga]]

Rosemary has brown slightly mousy hair that is always kept neatly pinned out of the way, and a straight, upright posture learned from years under Ivy’s careful guidance—a habit from Ivy’s own strict convent upbringing. Though still young, there is a depth in her eyes, a slightly too-old look that hints at having a strange childhood with little time spent among other children. She is kind-hearted, openly compassionate, and far more talkative than Ivy, her curiosity and warmth a natural counterpoint to the older woman’s measured reserve.

**Age** - 9
**Likes** - sweet smells, bread and jam, adventure stories, hot chocolate
**Dislikes** - animals suffering, Ivy’s long dry herbal tomes, being told off
